# Starting a Manual Labor Company in Fayetteville, Arkansas
## A Realistic Roadmap for Launching a Junk Removal & Lawn Care Business

---

## 1. Legal Formation

### Form an LLC (Recommended)
- **Why an LLC?** Separates your personal assets from business liability, looks professional to clients, and is straightforward to maintain in Arkansas.
- **Cost:** Arkansas Secretary of State LLC filing fee is approximately **$50** (online) or **$45** (paper).
- **How to file:** Visit [sos.arkansas.gov](https://www.sos.arkansas.gov) → Business & Commercial Services → LLC Filing.
- **Articles of Organization:** You'll provide the LLC name, registered agent, and principal address.
- **Name requirements:** Must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company." Check name availability on the SOS website before filing.
- **Processing time:** Online filings are typically processed in 2–5 business days.

### Registered Agent
- Every Arkansas LLC must have a registered agent with a physical Arkansas address (no P.O. boxes).
- You can serve as your own registered agent if you have a physical address in Arkansas.
- Alternatively, use a registered agent service (e.g., Northwest Registered Agent, Incfile) for approximately **$49–$99/year** — useful if you want to keep your home address private.

### EIN (Employer Identification Number)
- Apply free at [irs.gov/ein](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online).
- You'll need this to open a business bank account, hire employees, and file taxes.
- Takes about 10 minutes online; EIN is issued immediately.

### Operating Agreement
- Arkansas does not require LLCs to file an operating agreement, but you should have one.
- If operating solo, a simple single-member operating agreement is sufficient.
- Download a free template from LegalZoom or SCORE, or have a local attorney draft one (~$150–$300).

## 2. Business Licenses

### City of Fayetteville Business License
- **Required for:** Any business operating within Fayetteville city limits.
- **Application:** Submit to the City of Fayetteville Finance & Internal Services Department.
- **Cost:** Varies by business type; typically **$50–$150** for service businesses.
- **Renewal:** Annual, due January 1 of each year.
- **Where:** City Hall, 113 W. Mountain St., Fayetteville, AR 72701, or online at [fayetteville-ar.gov](https://www.fayetteville-ar.gov).

### Washington County Requirements
- Washington County does not currently require a separate county business license for most service businesses operating inside incorporated cities.
- If you operate in unincorporated areas of Washington County, check with the Washington County Judge's office at (479) 444-1711.

### State of Arkansas Requirements
- Arkansas does not require a general state business license for junk removal or lawn care.
- If you have employees, register with Arkansas Department of Workforce Services for unemployment insurance.
- Sales tax: Lawn care services are generally **not subject to Arkansas sales tax**. Junk removal hauling services are typically not taxed, but disposal of certain materials may have fees. Confirm with the Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration (DFA) at dfa.arkansas.gov.

### Contractor's License
- Junk removal and basic lawn care do not require a contractor's license in Arkansas.
- If you expand into landscaping (grading, irrigation, retaining walls), you may need an Arkansas Contractor's License from the Arkansas Contractors Licensing Board.

## 3. Insurance

### General Liability Insurance
- **Minimum recommended:** $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate.
- **What it covers:** Property damage, bodily injury to third parties, completed operations.
- **Why it matters:** Clients will ask for proof of insurance. Without it, you cannot work for property management companies or HOAs.
- **Estimated cost:** **$600–$1,500/year** for a sole proprietor or small LLC doing junk removal and lawn care.
- **Recommended providers:** Progressive Commercial, Hiscox, Next Insurance (get an online quote in minutes), or contact a local Fayetteville agent.

### Workers' Compensation Insurance
- **Required in Arkansas** if you have 3 or more employees (including part-time).
- Even with 1–2 employees, consider it to protect against employee injury claims.
- **Cost:** Approximately **$8–$15 per $100 of payroll** for manual labor classifications.
- Contact the Arkansas Workers' Compensation Commission at (501) 682-3930 for guidance.

### Commercial Auto Insurance
- If you use your personal truck for business, your personal auto policy likely does **not** cover commercial use.
- **Commercial auto policy cost:** **$1,200–$2,400/year** depending on vehicle value and coverage.
- If you purchase a box truck or dump trailer, insure it separately.

### Inland Marine / Equipment Insurance
- Covers tools and equipment (trailers, mowers, hand tools) if stolen or damaged.
- Often bundled with general liability; add-on cost is approximately **$200–$500/year**.

### Umbrella Policy
- Consider a $1M commercial umbrella policy (~$300–$500/year) once revenue exceeds $50K to protect against catastrophic claims.

## 5. Pricing Strategy for NW Arkansas Market

### Junk Removal Pricing
NW Arkansas is a growing, price-sensitive but quality-conscious market. Price based on truck/trailer volume.

| Load Size | Suggested Price |
|-----------|----------------|
| Minimum (small items, 1/8 truck) | $75–$125 |
| Quarter load | $150–$200 |
| Half load | $225–$275 |
| Full truck/trailer load | $350–$500 |
| Extra heavy items (appliances, pianos) | +$50–$100 each |
| Same-day service premium | +$25–$50 |

- **Labor rate base:** $75–$100/hour for 1 person. Add $40–$60/hour per additional crew member.
- **Dump fees:** Pass through to client at cost + small markup, or build into the estimate.
- **Competitors to research:** Junk King (Rogers), 1-800-GOT-JUNK (Fayetteville), and local Facebook Marketplace operators.

### Lawn Care Pricing
| Service | Fayetteville Market Rate |
|---------|------------------------|
| Standard mow & edge (small lot, <5,000 sq ft) | $40–$60 |
| Standard mow & edge (medium lot, 5,000–10,000 sq ft) | $60–$80 |
| Large lot / heavy growth | $80–$130 |
| Hedge / shrub trimming | $60–$150 depending on size |
| Mulch installation (per yard installed) | $75–$100 |
| Spring/fall cleanup | $150–$400 |
| Monthly maintenance contracts | Discount 5–10% from per-visit rate |

- **Recurring clients are your most valuable asset.** Weekly or bi-weekly lawn clients provide predictable cash flow.
- **Pricing tip:** Don't undercut by 50% to win jobs. Compete on reliability, communication, and professionalism, not price.

## 6. Marketing

### Google Business Profile (Free — Do This First)
- Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and create a profile.
- Verify your business via postcard (takes ~5 days to Fayetteville addresses).
- Add your services, service area, photos, and business hours.
- Ask every satisfied client to leave a Google review. This is your most powerful marketing tool.

### Nextdoor
- Create a business account at [nextdoor.com/business](https://nextdoor.com/business).
- Fayetteville and surrounding neighborhoods (Johnson, Prairie Grove, Springdale) are very active on Nextdoor.
- Post free updates and respond promptly to neighbor requests for recommendations.

### Facebook Marketplace and Groups
- List your services in Facebook Marketplace under "Services."
- Join local groups: "Fayetteville AR Buy Sell Trade," "NWA Community," "Fayetteville AR Neighborhood" groups.
- Post before/after photos of completed jobs (always ask client permission first).
- Run a targeted Facebook ad for $5–$10/day targeting homeowners age 30–65 in Washington County.

### Door Hangers and Yard Signs
- Print 500 door hangers (~$80–$120 from GotPrint or VistaPrint).
- Focus on neighborhoods in Fayetteville you want to serve: Dickson Street area, Gulley Park, Kessler Mountain, West Fayetteville.
- Leave a yard sign after every job (with client permission) for 1 week.

### Referral Program
- Offer existing clients $20–$25 credit toward their next service for each new client they refer who books a job.
- Word of mouth in Fayetteville/NWA is extremely effective due to strong community ties.

### Website (Optional in Year 1)
- A simple Squarespace or Wix site with your services, area served, contact form, and Google reviews widget is sufficient.
- Cost: $16–$23/month.
- Domain: ~$12/year from Namecheap or Google Domains.
- In year 1, Google Business Profile + Nextdoor will generate more leads than a website.

## 7. Operations

### Hiring
- For junk removal, you'll likely need 1–2 helpers per job. Consider:
  - 1099 contractors for sporadic work (confirm IRS worker classification rules).
  - W-2 part-time employees once workload is consistent (3+ days/week).
- Post on Indeed, Facebook Jobs, and Handshake (for UA Fayetteville students looking for physical work).
- Pay rate for helpers: $14–$18/hour starting in the Fayetteville market (competitive with warehouse/retail).
- **Screen carefully:** Do a working interview (paid) before committing to regular employment.

### Scheduling
- Use this LaborCo Management System to schedule appointments and manage clients.
- Batch appointments geographically to reduce drive time. Group Fayetteville South jobs on Tuesdays, North on Thursdays, etc.
- Confirm every appointment via text 24 hours in advance. Reduce no-shows dramatically.
- Allow 30-minute buffer between jobs initially; tighten as you get faster.

### Customer Communication
- **Response time matters.** Reply to all inquiries within 2 hours during business hours.
- Send a text/email confirmation after booking with: date, time window, what to expect, your phone number.
- Follow up after every job with a short message: "Thanks for choosing LaborCo! Please leave us a Google review if you were happy with the service: [link]"
- Handle complaints immediately and generously. A refund or discount on the next service costs far less than a 1-star review.

### Uniforms and Professionalism
- Match t-shirts or polo shirts with your company name (~$8–$15/shirt screen-printed, minimum 12).
- Clean truck and trailer — parked outside a client's home, this is free advertising.
- Wear gloves on every job. It signals professionalism and protects you.

## 8. Financial

### Business Bank Account
- Open a separate business checking account the day your EIN arrives.
- **Good options in Fayetteville:** Signature Bank of Arkansas (local, small business friendly), Arvest Bank, or an online option like Relay Financial (no fees, excellent for small businesses).
- Never commingle personal and business funds — it pierces your LLC liability protection.

### Accepting Payments
- **Venmo Business, Cash App Business:** Free to set up; clients love the convenience.
- **Square or Stripe:** Accept credit/debit cards via your phone. Processing fee: ~2.6% + $0.10/transaction.
- **Zelle:** Free bank-to-bank, instant. Works great for repeat clients.
- **Checks:** Still widely used in the 40+ demographic in NWA. Acceptable for invoiced work.
- Set a policy: Residential jobs paid same day (before truck leaves or upon job completion). Commercial clients can be Net 15 or Net 30 with a signed agreement.

### Bookkeeping
- Record every transaction from day one.
- Use this LaborCo Management System for invoicing, or export to:
  - **Wave Accounting** (free, cloud-based, excellent for small service businesses)
  - **QuickBooks Simple Start** (~$18/month)
- Track these categories separately: Revenue, Dump Fees, Fuel, Equipment, Insurance, Marketing, Labor.
- Keep all receipts — photograph them with Google Drive or Expensify.

### Quarterly Estimated Taxes
- As a self-employed LLC owner, you are responsible for quarterly estimated tax payments to the IRS.
- **Due dates:** April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15.
- **Estimate:** Set aside 25–30% of net profit for federal + Arkansas state income tax.
- Arkansas state income tax rate: Graduated, up to 4.7% for individuals (as of 2024).
- Self-employment tax (Social Security + Medicare): 15.3% on net earnings.
- Pay federal estimates at [irs.gov/payments](https://www.irs.gov/payments) (EFTPS system, free).

### Mileage Deduction
- Track every business mile driven.
- 2024 IRS standard mileage rate: 67 cents/mile.
- Use a free app like MileIQ or TripLog to automatically track mileage.
- On $40,000 in driving at 67¢/mile, that's potentially $26,800 in deductions.

## 9. Growth Milestones

### Month 1–3: Foundation
- [ ] LLC filed and approved
- [ ] EIN obtained, business bank account opened
- [ ] General liability insurance active
- [ ] Google Business Profile created and verified
- [ ] First 5 clients acquired (friends, family, Nextdoor)
- [ ] Complete 15–25 jobs, learn what you're good at and what to improve
- [ ] Target revenue: **$2,500–$5,000/month**

### Month 3–6: Building Momentum
- [ ] Consistent 4–6 recurring lawn care clients (predictable revenue)
- [ ] Established relationships with 1–2 property managers or real estate agents for referrals
- [ ] Process refined: scheduling, invoicing, follow-up texts automated
- [ ] Hired first part-time helper for bigger junk removal jobs
- [ ] 20+ Google reviews
- [ ] Target revenue: **$5,000–$8,000/month**

### Month 6–12: Growth Phase
- [ ] 15–20 recurring lawn care accounts
- [ ] 8–12 junk removal jobs per month
- [ ] Second vehicle or trailer acquired (or equipment upgraded)
- [ ] Regular part-time employee (W-2) for reliability
- [ ] Simple website live with booking form
- [ ] First year revenue target: **$60,000–$90,000**

### Year 2+: Scaling
- [ ] Hire a full-time crew lead so you can take on a sales/operations role
- [ ] Expand service area to Springdale, Rogers, Bentonville (NWA is one of the fastest-growing metros in the US)
- [ ] Consider specialization: estate cleanouts, commercial grounds maintenance contracts, or moving junk + donations to Habitat for Humanity ReStore
- [ ] Fleet of 2+ trucks, consistent brand presence
- [ ] Revenue target: **$150,000–$250,000+/year** with a 2-person operation
- [ ] Explore purchasing a dump trailer (~$4,000–$8,000 used) to eliminate dump fee pass-throughs
